# Phase Guide

Six biologically distinct phases from preconception through lactation, each with its own measurement priorities, supplementation focus, and biological events. Phase transitions trigger reassessment per the per-phase schedule below.

## Phase Definitions

| Phase | Timing | Key Biology | Transition Trigger |
|-------|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Pre-conception | 5-6 months before trying | Egg maturation (~3 months), building nutrient stores, establishing baseline | User decides to start trying |
| First trimester | Weeks 1-13 | Organogenesis (all major organs form), neural tube closure (days 22-28), placenta forming | Positive pregnancy test or confirmed conception date |
| Second trimester | Weeks 14-26 | Rapid fetal growth, bone formation, blood volume expansion (+50%), brain myelination begins | Calendar (week 14) |
| Third trimester | Weeks 27-40 | Brain development peaks, DHA accretion highest (weeks 35-40), fetal iron store accumulation | Calendar (week 27) |
| Postpartum | Delivery through ~6 weeks | Recovery from blood loss, hormone transition, milk production begins | Delivery |
| Lactation | ~6 weeks through weaning + recovery | Ongoing milk production, maternal store rebuilding, bone density recovery | Ongoing until weaning; recovery continues 18+ months |

## Why Pre-Conception Starts at 5-6 Months

RBC folate reflects tissue status, not just recent intake, and takes ~20 weeks to reach the neural tube protection threshold of 1050 nmol/L. Starting only 3 months before conception risks inadequate folate when the neural tube closes at days 22-28 — often before pregnancy is recognized. Red blood cells live ~120 days, so folate status reflects a rolling ~4-month average.

## Measurement Schedule by Phase

### Pre-conception (5-6 months before trying)

**Baseline panel**: All Tier 1 markers (see `optimal-ranges.md`). Add Tier 2 based on history.
**Recheck at baseline + 12 weeks**: RBC folate (verify tissue normalization), 25-OH vitamin D (verify supplementation response), homocysteine (verify reduction if elevated), ferritin (verify stores building), TSH (if levothyroxine started).

### First trimester (weeks 1-13)

**At first prenatal visit (~8-10 weeks)**: CBC, ferritin + TSAT, TSH + free T4, 25-OH vitamin D, RBC folate (confirm >1050), urinary iodine.
**Trimester-specific TSH range**: 0.1-2.5 mIU/mL (hCG suppresses TSH; this is normal).

### Second trimester (weeks 14-26)

**~20 weeks**: CBC (Hb <10.5 = anemia, not just dilution), ferritin, TSH (range: 0.2-3.0), 25-OH vitamin D if dose adjusted.
**24-28 weeks**: Glucose tolerance test (standard GDM screening).

### Third trimester (weeks 27-40)

**~32-34 weeks**: CBC (Hb <10 = treat), ferritin (last meaningful chance to build fetal iron stores), TSH (range: 0.3-3.0), B12 if borderline at baseline.

### Postpartum (delivery through ~6 weeks)

**48 hours post-delivery**: CBC + ferritin. Note: ferritin falsely elevated for first ~6 weeks due to inflammation; use Hb + clinical symptoms.
**Critical finding**: Low ferritin at 48 hours predicts ~4x postpartum depression risk.

### Lactation (6 weeks through weaning + recovery)

**~3 months postpartum**: Ferritin (first reliable reading post-inflammation), TSH (postpartum thyroiditis hyperthyroid phase peaks ~14 weeks), 25-OH vitamin D, CBC.
**~6 months postpartum**: TSH (hypothyroid phase peaks ~19 weeks; ~30% develop permanent hypothyroidism), ferritin, B12 if vegan/vegetarian or borderline at baseline.
