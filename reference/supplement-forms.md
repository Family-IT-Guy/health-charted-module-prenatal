# Supplement Form Recommendations

Different forms of the same nutrient have meaningfully different absorption and tolerability. Form choice often matters more than nominal dose at the label.

## Preferred Forms

| Nutrient | Preferred Form | Avoid | Why |
|----------|---------------|-------|-----|
| Folate | Methylfolate (5-MTHF) or folinic acid | Folic acid (not unsafe, but inferior) | Methylfolate achieves ~4x higher serum levels, produces zero unmetabolized folic acid, works regardless of MTHFR status |
| Iron | Ferrous bisglycinate (chelated) | Ferrous sulfate | Bisglycinate: better absorbed, significantly fewer GI side effects (nausea, constipation) |
| B12 | Methylcobalamin | Cyanocobalamin (not unsafe, but inferior) | Methylcobalamin is the active form; cyanocobalamin requires conversion and releases trace cyanide |
| Vitamin D | Cholecalciferol (D3) | Ergocalciferol (D2) | D3 is ~2-3x more effective at raising serum 25(OH)D |
| Vitamin A | Beta-carotene | Retinol / retinyl palmitate in high doses | Beta-carotene conversion is self-limiting (cannot cause toxicity). Retinol >10,000 IU is teratogenic. See [[vitamin-a-teratogenicity-mechanism]]. |
| Calcium | Calcium citrate | Calcium carbonate (if low stomach acid) | Citrate absorbed without stomach acid; carbonate requires acidic environment |
| Magnesium | Magnesium glycinate or malate | Magnesium oxide | Oxide has ~4% bioavailability; glycinate is better absorbed and tolerated |
| Omega-3 | Triglyceride-form DHA from microalgae or fish oil | Ethyl ester form | Triglyceride form has ~70% better absorption |
| Choline | Choline bitartrate or choline chloride | - | Both well-absorbed; phosphatidylcholine also acceptable |
| Iodine | Potassium iodide | Kelp-derived (variable potency) | Kelp iodine content is inconsistent; KI is standardized |
| Selenium | Selenomethionine | Sodium selenite | Selenomethionine has higher bioavailability and better tissue retention |

## Evaluating a Supplement Product

When evaluating a specific product, check:

1. **Which FORMS** of each nutrient? (see table above)
2. **Which DOSES?** (compare against pregnancy-phase RDA and UL — see `safety-rules.md`)
3. **What's MISSING?** (choline is the most commonly under-dosed or omitted nutrient in prenatals)
4. **What's OVERLAPPING** with the user's other supplements? (especially vitamin D, K2, B12, B6)
5. **DELIVERY FORMAT?** (capsule count, liquid, powder — match user tolerance and adherence)
6. **Third-party tested?** (USP, NSF, or ConsumerLab certification verifies label accuracy and purity, but does NOT verify bioavailability or efficacy)
