Clinically meaningful interactions based on RCT evidence. Apply when the user's stack includes multiple supplements that may compete or synergize.

## Inhibitory Interactions (separate by 2+ hours)

| Nutrient A | Nutrient B | Effect | Evidence |
|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Iron | Calcium | Calcium reduces iron absorption ~30-50% | Strong (multiple RCTs) |
| Iron | Zinc | Bidirectional competition at supplemental doses | Moderate |
| Iron | Magnesium | Competition for absorption | Moderate |
| Levothyroxine | Iron | Iron reduces levothyroxine absorption | Strong |
| Levothyroxine | Calcium | Calcium reduces levothyroxine absorption | Strong |

## Synergistic Interactions (take together)

| Nutrient A | Nutrient B | Effect | Evidence |
|-----------|-----------|--------|----------|
| Iron | Vitamin C | Vitamin C increases non-heme iron absorption ~300-400% | Strong |
| Vitamin D | Calcium | Vitamin D required for calcium absorption | Strong |
| Folate | B12 + B6 | One-carbon metabolism requires all three simultaneously | Strong |

## Practical Daily Timing Template

Adjust based on the user's actual stack. The principle: iron alone with vitamin C in the morning, prenatal with meals midday, calcium (if supplementing) in the evening.

```
Morning (empty stomach or with vitamin C source):
- Iron (if supplementing)
- Levothyroxine (if taking; 30-60 min before food and 4 hours from iron/calcium)

With breakfast/lunch:
- Prenatal multivitamin (contains calcium, magnesium, zinc - away from iron)
- DHA/omega-3 (fat-soluble, take with food)
- Vitamin D (fat-soluble, take with food - unless in prenatal)

Evening (2+ hours after prenatal):
- Standalone calcium (if supplementing beyond prenatal)
- Magnesium (can help sleep)

Any time:
- Choline (no significant interactions)
- Probiotics (generally with food, but flexible)
```
