# Neural tube closure window

Biological window. Days 22-28 of gestation, when the neural tube closes irreversibly. Folate adequacy at this window determines neural tube defect risk. RBC folate >1050 nmol/L is the prevention threshold; reaching that level requires ~20 weeks of methylfolate intake, which is why preconception folate adequacy matters more than first-trimester start.
