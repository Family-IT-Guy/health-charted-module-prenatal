# Fetal Iron Store Window

Biological window. Weeks ~28-40 when fetal iron stores accumulate. Maternal-iron status during this window determines infant iron status for the first 4-6 months of life (during which breast-milk iron alone is insufficient). Last meaningful chance to build maternal ferritin before delivery is ~32-34 weeks.
