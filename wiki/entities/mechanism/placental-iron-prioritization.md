# Placental Iron Prioritization

Mechanism. The placenta concentrates iron via transferrin receptors and transfers it preferentially to the fetus, even when maternal iron status is depleted. Maternal anemia at delivery does not necessarily imply fetal iron inadequacy at birth, but does set up postpartum maternal depletion and early-infancy fetal iron-store insufficiency.
