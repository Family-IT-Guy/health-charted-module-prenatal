# Vitamin A Teratogenicity Mechanism

Mechanism. Excess preformed retinol overwhelms CYP26A1 degradation, producing supraphysiological retinoic acid that disrupts neural-crest-cell migration and HOX-gene expression. Beta-carotene is exempt because conversion to retinol is self-limiting and saturates well below teratogenic plasma levels. Threshold for teratogenicity is approximately 10,000 IU retinol/day.
