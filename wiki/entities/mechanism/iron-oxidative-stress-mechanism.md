# Iron Oxidative Stress Mechanism

Mechanism. Free iron catalyzes Fenton chemistry — conversion of hydrogen peroxide to hydroxyl radicals — generating oxidative stress that damages lipids, proteins, and DNA. Hepcidin downregulates iron absorption when stores are adequate, but high-dose oral iron in non-deficient patients can saturate this protective mechanism. Weekly dosing avoids most of this in prophylactic supplementation.
