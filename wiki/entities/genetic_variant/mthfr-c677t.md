# MTHFR C677T

Genetic variant. Single-nucleotide polymorphism in the MTHFR gene affecting methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase activity. Homozygous TT genotype reduces enzyme activity to ~30% of wild-type, impairing folic-acid-to-methylfolate conversion. Methylfolate (5-MTHF) supplementation bypasses the reduced-activity step.
