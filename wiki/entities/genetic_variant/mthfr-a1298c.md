# Mthfr A1298C

Genetic variant. Second-most-studied MTHFR SNP. Reduces enzyme activity less than C677T; clinical significance for prenatal nutrition is most relevant in compound heterozygotes (C677T/A1298C). Methylfolate supplementation bypasses the affected step.
