# Hfe Genotype

Genetic variant. HFE-gene variants (C282Y, H63D) associated with hereditary hemochromatosis (iron overload). Carriers and homozygotes should avoid routine iron supplementation; high ferritin in this context is a signal of overload, not adequacy.
