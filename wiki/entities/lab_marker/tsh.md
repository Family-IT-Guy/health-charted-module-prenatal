# Tsh

Lab marker. Pituitary hormone regulating thyroid function. Pregnancy-specific ranges: T1 0.1-2.5 (hCG suppresses), T2 0.2-3.0, T3 0.3-3.0 mIU/mL. Marginal elevation in pregnancy is consequential because thyroid demand increases ~50%.
