# Free T3

Lab marker. Free (unbound) triiodothyronine; the most metabolically active thyroid hormone fraction. Pregnancy-optimal 2.3-4.2 pg/mL. Useful when TSH is borderline or when investigating T4-to-T3 conversion adequacy (relevant in selenium deficiency or with deiodinase polymorphisms).
