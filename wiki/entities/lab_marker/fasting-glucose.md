# Fasting Glucose

Lab marker. Plasma glucose after 8+ hour fast. Pregnancy-optimal <100 mg/dL; >100 indicates impaired fasting glucose. Different role from the glucose-tolerance test used for GDM screening at 24-28 weeks.
