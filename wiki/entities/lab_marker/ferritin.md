# Ferritin

Lab marker. Iron-storage protein. Pregnancy-optimal range 50-100 mcg/L (minimum 30); standard population reference range (15-200 mcg/L) is inadequate for pregnancy. Acute-phase reactant — rises with inflammation independent of iron status.
