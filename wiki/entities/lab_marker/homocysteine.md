# Homocysteine

Lab marker. Amino-acid intermediate in one-carbon metabolism. Elevation indicates functional impairment of methylation (folate, B12, B6 cofactor inadequacy) and is independently associated with pregnancy-loss risk above ~12 mcmol/L.
