# Rbc Folate

Lab marker. Reflects tissue folate status over the prior ~120 days (red-cell lifespan), unlike serum folate which reflects only recent intake. Pregnancy-optimal threshold >1050 nmol/L, the level associated with ~83% reduction in neural tube defect risk.
