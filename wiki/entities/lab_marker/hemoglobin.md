# Hemoglobin

Lab marker. Oxygen-transport protein in red blood cells. Pregnancy reference targets are lower than non-pregnant ranges due to plasma-volume expansion (~50%); pregnancy-optimal preconception >12 g/dL with anemia thresholds of <10.5 g/dL in T2 and <10 g/dL in T3.
