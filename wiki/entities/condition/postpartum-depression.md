# Postpartum Depression

Condition. Mood disorder occurring after delivery. Risk roughly quadruples with low ferritin at 48 hours postpartum and roughly doubles (2.28x) with vitamin D deficiency. EPA supplementation shows the largest mood-effect size in supplementation trials.
