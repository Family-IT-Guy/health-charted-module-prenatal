# Gestational Diabetes Mellitus

Condition. Pregnancy-onset glucose intolerance, screened at 24-28 weeks. Prior-pregnancy GDM substantially increases recurrence risk; long-term type 2 diabetes risk roughly doubles. Nutritional management is first-line.
