# Postpartum thyroiditis

Condition. Autoimmune-driven thyroid dysfunction occurring in ~8% of women after delivery. Hyperthyroid phase peaks ~14 weeks postpartum, hypothyroid phase peaks ~19 weeks. Approximately 30% develop permanent hypothyroidism. TPO-antibody-positive women are at substantially elevated risk; selenium supplementation reduces incidence in TPO+ populations.
