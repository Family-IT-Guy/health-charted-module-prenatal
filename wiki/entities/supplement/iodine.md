# Iodine

Supplement. Required for thyroid hormone synthesis. Demand increases ~50% in pregnancy. Narrowest therapeutic window of any pregnancy nutrient (target ~150-250 mcg supplemental; UL 1100 mcg). Most sensitive breast-milk nutrient — infant entirely dependent on maternal supply.
