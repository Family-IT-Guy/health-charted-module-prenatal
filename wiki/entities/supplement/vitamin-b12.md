# Vitamin B12

Supplement. Cobalamin. Required for one-carbon metabolism, neural-tube closure (alongside folate), and erythropoiesis. Active form (methylcobalamin) is preferred over cyanocobalamin; deficiency is common in vegan/vegetarian patterns.
