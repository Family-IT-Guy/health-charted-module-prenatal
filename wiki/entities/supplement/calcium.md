# Calcium

Supplement. Major mineral required for fetal skeletal development and maternal bone preservation. Target ~1000 mg/day total (diet + supplements). Citrate form preferred over carbonate when stomach acid is reduced. Significant interaction with iron and levothyroxine absorption.
