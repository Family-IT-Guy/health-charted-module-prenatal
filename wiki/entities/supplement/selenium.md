# Selenium

Supplement. Trace mineral and component of selenoproteins involved in thyroid hormone metabolism. Dose 200 mcg/day reduces postpartum thyroiditis from ~48.6% to ~28.6% in TPO+ patients. Selenomethionine form preferred. UL 400 mcg.
