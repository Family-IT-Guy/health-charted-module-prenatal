# Vitamin B6

Supplement. B-vitamin required as cofactor in one-carbon metabolism and neurotransmitter synthesis. Used at low doses (10-25 mg) for nausea management in T1. UL 100 mg/day; chronic high-dose causes peripheral neuropathy.
