# Magnesium

Supplement. Cofactor for ~300 enzymes including those in vitamin D activation and energy metabolism. Glycinate or malate forms preferred; oxide has ~4% bioavailability. Supplemental UL 350 mg/day; dietary magnesium has no UL.
