# Iodine narrow margin

Concept. Iodine has the narrowest therapeutic window of any pregnancy nutrient. Recommended supplemental dose 150-250 mcg/day; UL 1100 mcg/day; values below ~100 mcg dietary intake produce maternal-fetal hypothyroxinemia, while values above the UL can induce thyroid dysfunction. Stay-within constraint applies on both sides, unlike most pregnancy nutrients where insufficiency is the main risk.
