# Vegan and vegetarian pattern implications

Concept. Plant-based dietary patterns shift baseline nutrient adequacy in ways that affect pregnancy supplementation choices. Vegans require routine B12 supplementation (animal-product absent diet); choline intake tends to be lower (eggs and meat are top sources); long-chain omega-3 (DHA/EPA) requires algal supplementation (no direct intake from plant sources, and ALA conversion is limited and further reduced by some genetic variants); zinc and iron may require attention (plant sources have lower bioavailability with phytate inhibition); B6 and B2 are typically adequate.
