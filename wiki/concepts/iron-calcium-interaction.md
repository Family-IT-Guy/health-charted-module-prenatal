# Iron-calcium interaction

Concept. Calcium reduces iron absorption by 30-50% through shared intestinal transporters. Clinically meaningful at supplemental doses; the practical implication is to separate iron-supplementation timing from calcium-containing meals or supplements by 2+ hours.
