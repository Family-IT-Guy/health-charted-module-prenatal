# One Carbon Metabolism Cycle

Concept. Interlocking biochemical cycles in which folate, B12, B6, and choline donate or recycle methyl groups required for DNA synthesis, neurotransmitter production, and gene-expression methylation. Functional adequacy requires all four nutrients simultaneously; single-nutrient adequacy can be insufficient.
