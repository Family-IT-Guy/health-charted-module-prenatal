# Group I vs Group II breast milk nutrients

Concept. Two-class framework for understanding how maternal nutrient status maps to infant intake during lactation. Group I nutrients (vitamin D, DHA, iodine, selenium, B vitamins, choline, vitamins A/E/K) are diet-dependent — milk concentration tracks maternal intake. Group II nutrients (calcium, iron, zinc, folate, copper) are diet-independent — milk concentration is preserved at maternal expense, so the mother depletes if not replenished.
