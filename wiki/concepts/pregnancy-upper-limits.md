# Pregnancy Upper Limits

Concept. Pregnancy-specific tolerable upper intake levels for supplemental nutrients. Most pregnancy ULs match non-pregnant values (where evidence exists), but some are pregnancy-specific: vitamin A retinol 10,000 IU teratogenicity threshold; iodine 1100 mcg narrow margin; conservative D 4000 IU despite higher tolerated doses in lactation RCTs.
