# Ferritin Acute Phase Reactant Caveat

Concept. Ferritin rises with inflammation independent of iron status. In patients with infection, autoimmune conditions, or in the first ~6 weeks postpartum, normal ferritin can mask iron depletion. Confirm with transferrin saturation and clinical context before declaring iron adequacy.
