# Supplement Form Bioavailability

Concept. Different chemical forms of the same nutrient have meaningfully different absorption, tissue distribution, and downstream activity. Form choice often matters more than nominal label dose: methylfolate vs folic acid, methyl-B12 vs cyanocobalamin, ferrous bisglycinate vs sulfate, magnesium glycinate vs oxide, triglyceride DHA vs ethyl ester.
