# Maternal Depletion Syndrome

Concept. Cumulative micronutrient depletion across pregnancy and lactation when maternal repletion does not match outflow. Especially severe for Group II breast-milk nutrients (calcium, iron, zinc, folate, copper) where maternal stores are sacrificed to maintain milk content. Underlies WHO's 18-24 month inter-pregnancy interval recommendation.
