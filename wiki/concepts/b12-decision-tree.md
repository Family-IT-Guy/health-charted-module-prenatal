# B12 decision tree

Concept. Tiered testing approach for vitamin B12 in pregnancy. Serum B12 alone is insufficient because the 200-400 pg/mL "gray zone" hides functional deficiency in ~5-10% of cases. Decision tree: serum B12 >400 → replete; 200-400 → order holoTC and MMA (holoTC <25 OR MMA elevated indicates functional deficiency despite normal serum B12); <200 → deficient, supplement immediately.
