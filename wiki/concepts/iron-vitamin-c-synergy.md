# Iron Vitamin C Synergy

Concept. Vitamin C increases non-heme iron absorption ~300-400% by reducing ferric iron to absorbable ferrous form and forming a soluble complex. Practical application: take iron supplements with a vitamin C source on an empty stomach for maximum absorption when supplementing.
