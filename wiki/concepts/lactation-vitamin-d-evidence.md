# Lactation Vitamin D Evidence

Concept. RCT evidence (Hollis et al., 2015, n=334) that 6,400 IU/day maternal vitamin D supplementation raises breast-milk vitamin D ~10-fold to levels equivalent to direct infant 400 IU supplementation. Standard prenatal doses (400-2000 IU) do NOT produce adequate milk levels. The trial is the basis for distinct lactation dosing relative to pregnancy.
