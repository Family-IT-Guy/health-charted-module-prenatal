# Thyroid autoimmunity and pregnancy

Concept. Thyroid autoantibodies (TPO antibodies, thyroglobulin antibodies) modulate pregnancy and postpartum thyroid risk independently of TSH status. Euthyroid TPO-positive patients carry approximately 2x miscarriage risk and elevated postpartum-thyroiditis risk. Selenium 200 mcg/day reduces postpartum-thyroiditis incidence in this population from ~48.6% to ~28.6%. Surveillance cadence: TSH and free T4 trimester-specific ranges; postpartum thyroid screen at 3 and 6 months.
