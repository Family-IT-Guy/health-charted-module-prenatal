# Pregnancy optimal vs standard ranges

Concept. Standard population reference ranges are designed to flag disease in non-pregnant adults; pregnancy-optimal ranges are calibrated for pregnancy outcomes. The two often disagree (ferritin, RBC folate, TSH, 25-OH-vitamin-D, hemoglobin, MCV). Applying standard "normal" ranges in pregnancy misses early deficiency and overlooks markers known to predict adverse outcomes.

This is a domain-specific application of HC core's Threshold-as-Claim Discipline — every range is itself a claim with its own evidence base, and ranges chosen for non-pregnant populations are not automatically valid in pregnancy context.
